Atlas of Why

Why the world looks the way it does — explained with maps and real data.

Atlas of Why is an educational YouTube channel about geography, economics, engineering and infrastructure. Every video is built from public data sources, which are cited in the video description.

YouTube channel: youtube.com/@atlas.ofwhytv

Atlas of Why publisher

"Atlas of Why publisher" is a private tool used only by the owner of the Atlas of Why channel to upload its own videos, set their titles, descriptions, thumbnails and schedule, and read the channel's own YouTube Analytics. It has no other users and does not access anyone else's data.

Narration in our videos is generated with AI voice tools; this is disclosed in each video.
